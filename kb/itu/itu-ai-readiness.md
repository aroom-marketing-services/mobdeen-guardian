# ITU AI Readiness Framework

## Relevance to the use case

The hackathon asks teams to consider open data and open-source management, the generative-AI content ecosystem, local and regional context, domain integration, human supervision, policy opportunities, social inclusion, and digital infrastructure.

## Mobdeen Guardian mapping

- Open management: public source register, hashes, and reproducible ingestion.
- Content ecosystem: source-grounded generation and age-appropriate output control.
- Context: Arabic family-learning use case and UAE policy sources.
- Domain integration: education and family services.
- Human supervision: guardian approval before child delivery.
- Policy opportunity: documented domain gaps with global comparisons.
- Inclusion: simple Arabic and an offline demonstration path.
- Infrastructure: optional external provider with a local fail-safe mode.
