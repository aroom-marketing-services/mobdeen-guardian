# ITU T Y 3172 Architectural Framework

## Pipeline mapping

The submission maps the use case to the machine-learning pipeline pattern used in the hackathon guidance: source, collector, preprocessor, model, policy, distributor, and sink. The machine-learning function orchestrator, sandbox, and human supervision govern the full flow.

## Mobdeen Guardian mapping

- SRC: supervising parent or child message.
- C: request intake with actor, audience, and declared purpose.
- PP: aliasing, redaction, age-band conversion, and risk classification.
- M: policy retrieval plus an optional text generator.
- P: deterministic child-safety, purpose-limitation, and guardian-review controls.
- D: response packaging with citations and action status.
- SINK: guardian review screen or blocked-and-escalated outcome.
- MLFO and sandbox: orchestration, provider isolation, service stop, usage limits, and audit events.
