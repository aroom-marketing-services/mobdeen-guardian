# UAE Charter for the Development and Use of Artificial Intelligence

## Relevance to the use case

The Charter frames responsible AI use around human well-being, privacy and data security, transparency, accountability, inclusion, and compliance with applicable laws and agreements. These principles are directly relevant to an AI assistant used by families and children.

## Controls implemented in the demo

- A supervising parent approves generated guidance before it reaches a child.
- The agent returns source references and a concise decision summary.
- High-risk or controversial uses stop and move to human review.
- The offline demonstration remains available without sending data to a third-party model.
