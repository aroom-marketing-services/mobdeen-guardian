# UAE Personal Data Protection Law

## Relevance to the use case

The UAE Personal Data Protection Law provides a national framework for protecting personal data and privacy. For Mobdeen Guardian, child and family information must be limited to what the requested guidance needs, protected during processing, and handled for a stated purpose.

## Controls implemented in the demo

- Replace child names with aliases before retrieval or model use.
- Remove phone numbers, email addresses, links, and direct location details.
- Store an input fingerprint rather than the raw child message in the audit log.
- Prevent secondary commercial use of child data within the scenario.
- Keep the supervising parent in the decision loop.

## Scope note

This summary supports a technical demonstration and is not legal advice. The official text and its current implementing requirements remain authoritative.
