# UAE Child Rights Law Wadeema

## Relevance to the use case

Federal Law No. 3 of 2016 concerning child rights establishes protections connected to children's safety, care, development, education, and non-discrimination. A family-learning agent should place the child's welfare and safe access to appropriate content ahead of engagement or commercial goals.

## Controls implemented in the demo

- Content is written for an age band and reviewed by a guardian.
- The agent does not ask a child for identifying or location information.
- A child who reports fear, harm, or a secret involving personal information receives a safe response and adult-support direction.
- Commercial targeting based on inferred vulnerability is blocked.
