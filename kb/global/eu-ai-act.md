# European Union Artificial Intelligence Act

## Relevance to the use case

The EU Artificial Intelligence Act uses a risk-based framework. Among its prohibited practices are certain materially harmful uses that exploit vulnerabilities related to age. It also includes transparency and governance obligations for relevant AI systems and models.

## Global practice used in the mapping

- Do not exploit a child's age-related vulnerability to influence behaviour in a harmful way.
- Document system purpose, controls, and human oversight.
- Provide appropriate transparency when a person interacts with AI.
- Monitor system behaviour and respond to identified risks.
