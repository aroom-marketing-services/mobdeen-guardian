# United States COPPA Rule

## Relevance to the use case

The United States Children's Online Privacy Protection Rule gives parents tools to control the collection of personal information from children under 13 by covered online services. It includes notice, verifiable parental consent, access, security, retention, and deletion expectations.

## Global practice used in the mapping

- Obtain parental permission before collecting covered child data where the rule applies.
- Give parents clear notice and control.
- Limit retention and protect stored information.
- Treat child-directed services as requiring stronger default safeguards.
