# UNICEF Guidance on AI and Children Version 3

## Relevance to the use case

UNICEF's guidance places children's rights at the centre of AI policy and system design. It addresses safety, privacy, fairness, transparency, inclusion, well-being, preparedness, and meaningful participation by children and caregivers.

## Global practice used in the mapping

- Evaluate the best interests and well-being of the child throughout the system lifecycle.
- Use child-centred safety and privacy by design.
- Give caregivers understandable information and meaningful controls.
- Monitor harms and provide accountable human escalation.
