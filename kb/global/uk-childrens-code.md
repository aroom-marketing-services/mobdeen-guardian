# United Kingdom Childrens Code

## Relevance to the use case

The UK Information Commissioner's Office Children's Code contains age-appropriate design standards for online services likely to be accessed by children. It treats the best interests of the child as a primary design consideration and expects privacy information and controls to suit the child's age and understanding.

## Global practice used in the mapping

- Apply high privacy settings by default.
- Minimize collected and retained data.
- Present notices in language suited to the child.
- Avoid nudges that weaken privacy choices.
- Establish age with certainty proportionate to risk or apply child protections broadly.
